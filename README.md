**rebye** (n.) — the second goodbye to someone you said goodbye to minutes earlier, after bumping into them again.

Everyone has lived it: you say goodbye at the lift, then meet the same person again by the car park, the corridor, or the front gate — and there was no word for that second, awkward farewell. *Rebye* is built from *re-* + *bye*, so it is sayable on first sight, spellable on first hearing, and impossible to forget once you have used it.

Example in use: "We said goodbye at the lift, then met again by the car park — cue the rebye."

`main.py` writes the word and its one-line definition (110 characters, within the 140-character limit) to `entry.txt`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
